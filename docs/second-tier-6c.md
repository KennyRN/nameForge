# Batch 6c: Indian, Chinese, Japanese and Korean (second-tier tables brief §13)

Generated by `scripts/make-doc-6c.mjs` from the word files of `scripts/tier6c/`; do not edit by hand. Every list is ten words in the fixed order (three ancient to medieval, three early modern to steam age, two steam age to modern, one fantastic from the culture's own lore, one for every era); a word after the tenth carries its own era.

## Exemptions and judgement calls

- **Deserts (Japanese and Korean):** Japan and Korea have no deserts of their own, so the desert module has no Japanese or Korean content: no noun list, no shapes. The exemption is marked in `lens.json` (`coverageExempt.desertNames`), the coverage report shows both cells as excluded, and `tests/tier6cCoverage.test.ts` holds that. (This is the brief's ruling for 6c; it differs from the 6b judgement, where every culture had a desert.) The Indian (the Thar) and Chinese (the Gobi, the Taklamakan) deserts are written at full depth.
- **Deferred styles:** stars and space, hyperspace charts, spacecraft and river names stay with the styles brief for the four cultures (`deferred.styles` in `lens.json`), as for every earlier culture.
- **Family names, Chinese, Japanese and Korean:** for these family-first cultures the module's own words are the native surnames (the native and mixed settings), and they count towards the module's target: each surname is a word and a form (the surname written first). English mode keeps only the translated subset (the 15 Chinese pairs and the Japanese and Korean surnames that have a plain English sense); no English surname is invented. Japanese has 37 common surnames and Korean 34 (30 or more each), the new Japanese ones with an English sense where it is plain.
- **Family names, Indian (exempt by design):** Indian family names are made from the son-of and daughter-of forms and place forms, so that no caste-marking surname gets in (the caste safeguard). The cell is marked `exempt: by design` (`coverageByDesign` in `lens.json`), not excluded: Indian stays in the family-names menu and makes names as it does.
- **Loanwords:** every loanword was looked up, and the results and the 18 swaps are in `docs/loanwords.md`.
