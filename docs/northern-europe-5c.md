# Northern Europe tables: batch 5c

For Kenny's review. Generated by `scripts/make-north5c.mjs` from `scripts/north5c/`: every signature word and frame the batch adds to the seven group-name families, tribes and kin groups, the three bynames modules, realms and polities, peoples and tongues, ships and boats, aircraft and airships and land vehicles (spacecraft is left to the style tables brief).

**How to read it.** Each module has three kinds of word, ten per kind for each of the seven core cultures (`{N0}`, `{N1}`, `{N2}` in a frame) and four for Celtic's own, and ten frames per culture (four for Celtic). `{A}` is one of the culture's adjectives; `[founder]`, `[place]` and `[holy person]` are filled from the people tables and settlement names. A culture's frames take 40% of the draws when it is chosen; Celtic also draws each nation's at a quarter weight. English (`english`) serves the Anglo-Saxon and Norman & British cultures together.

**Tags (5d §14.1).** A word in square brackets carries an era span (`AN-EM`, `ST-MO`, `MO`) and/or a trailing `*` for a fantastic word: it is drawn only where the state's eras meet the span and, if fantastic, only in a fantastic setting. A word with no tag belongs to every era. Titles also hold each culture's rank pairs (the man's form, the woman's): see `RANKS` in `scripts/north5c/content-ranks.mjs`.
