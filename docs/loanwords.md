# Loanwords

Every non-English word the Northern Europe batches 5a and 5b use, checked on collinsdictionary.com (only the word was sent; Oxford was not needed). A word counts as found when Collins has an entry for it in the sense the tables use; a word found only in another sense is treated as not found and replaced by its English sense. Wheal and Zawn stay, as the brief says (Cornish only).

- Words checked online: 167
- Kept (found in Collins): 150
- Replaced after the online check: 17
- Replaced earlier, on the memory check, and not looked up online: 10 (Ballybetagh, Cantred, Callow, Davoch, Fank, Fermtoun, Toun, Fianna, Heugh, Knott)
- Replaced in earlier fixes for being non-English or non-dictionary: Bal, Carn, Draugr, Autobahn, Barrow Wight.
