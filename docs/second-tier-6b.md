# Batch 6b review (second-tier tables brief §11)

## Part A: inn signs of their own

Every core-depth culture (the core cultures and the 6a cultures) draws its inn signs from lists in its own habits, rendered in English (src/data/inn-signs.json, generated by scripts/make-inn-signs.mjs from scripts/inn-signs-data.mjs): at least 12 creatures, 12 objects, 8 trades and 6 colours or metals, and also the persons, descriptions and places a sign is named after. The shared lists stay for the legacy mixes and for the cultures not yet at core depth. A setting's boosted shared words (a port's anchors and nets) still join a culture's own signs where the setting asks for them. The limit is `innLimit` 5 in lens.json `distinctness`: over seeds 1 to 5 no two core-depth cultures share more than 5% of their inn names at the same seed, every pair, in tests/distinctness.test.ts.

Before: 581 inn names shared at the same seed across the 153 pairs of the 18 cultures; 66 pairs over 5%; the largest pair Portuguese/Byzantine at 36.7%. After: 30 shared; none over 5%; the largest Welsh/Irish at 3.3% ("the Old Inn", "the Harp Rest House" before the Irish harp became a brooch). The Welsh and Cornish legacy weight of 0.02, set on 18 November for inns, is restored to 0.1: every limit holds without it.
