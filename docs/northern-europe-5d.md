# Northern Europe tables: batch 5d

For Kenny's review. Generated by `scripts/make-north5d.mjs` from `scripts/north5d/`: every signature word and frame the batch adds to money and coins, events and eras, calendars and festivals, sky lore, arms, treasures, books and deities.

**How to read it.** Each module has three kinds of word, ten per kind for each of the seven core cultures (`{N0}`, `{N1}`, `{N2}` in a frame) and four for Celtic's own, and ten frames per culture (four for Celtic). `{A}` is one of the culture's adjectives; `[founder]`, `[place]` and `[holy person]` are filled from the people tables and settlement names. A culture's frames take 40% of the draws when it is chosen; Celtic also draws each nation's at a quarter weight. English (`english`) serves the Anglo-Saxon and Norman & British cultures together.

**Tags.** A word in square brackets carries an era span (`AN-EM`, `ST-MO`, `MO`) and/or a trailing `*` for a fantastic word: it is drawn only where the state's eras meet the span and, if fantastic, only in a fantastic setting. A word with no tag belongs to every era. A frame of a calendar or a sky is written for one kind (a month, a festival, a moon; a constellation, a light) and drawn only for it.
