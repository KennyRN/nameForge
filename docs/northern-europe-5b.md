# Northern Europe tables: batch 5b

For Kenny's review. Generated by `scripts/make-north5b.mjs` from `scripts/north5b/`: every entry the batch adds to settlement names, streets and districts, inns and taverns, buildings and landmarks, monuments and statues (the settlement kit composes them and has no tables of its own).

**How to read it.** A word list reads `Word` with its eras after a semicolon. Each list has ten words in a fixed order: three of the ancient to medieval eras (`AN-MD`), three of the early modern to the steam age (`EM-ST`), two of the steam age to modern (`ST-MO`), one fantastic (`h`, the culture's own lore) and one for every era. Celtic holds eight words per list. A frame is a name shape: `{N0}`, `{N1}` and `{N2}` are the module's own nouns of its three main kinds.
